# User Guide - Asset Management System

## Overview
This system is an enterprise-grade Asset Management ERP designed to handle lifecycle tracking, maintenance, rentals, and financial reporting.

## Key Modules

### 1. Asset Management
- **Dashboard**: View real-time value, status distribution, and recent activities.
- **Inventory**: Track assets with details like Serial Number, Location, Department, and Condition.
- **Lifecycle**: Manage assignment, transfer, and disposal of assets.
- **Search**: fast full-text search by name, code, brand, or serial number.

### 2. Maintenance & Work Orders
- **Schedule**: Plan preventive maintenance or react to issues.
- **Work Orders**: Assign technicians, track costs, and managed approvals.
- **History**: View full maintenance history per asset.

### 3. Analytics & Reporting
- **Executive Dashboard**:
    - **Cost Trends**: Visualize monthly spending on maintenance and fuel.
    - **Asset Status**: Instant view of how many assets are Active, In Maintenance, or Rented.
- **Export**:
    - **PDF Inventory**: Download a formatted asset list for physical audits.
    - **CSV Exports**: Export raw data for maintenance logs and asset lists.

### 4. Settings & Configuration
- **Company Branding**: Upload your company logo to customize reports and the UI.
- **Parameters**: Configure system-wide settings.

## How-To Guides

### How to Add an Asset
1. Navigate to **Assets** > **New Asset**.
2. Fill in the required fields (Name, Category, Location).
3. (Optional) Add financial data (Purchase Price, Date).
4. Click **Save**.

### How to Request Maintenance
1. Go to **Work Orders**.
2. Click **New Work Order**.
3. Select the Asset and describe the issue.
4. Set priority and due date.

### How to Export Reports
1. Go to **Report Center**.
2. To export the full list, click **Export Inventory (PDF)** in the header.
3. For specific data, use the CSV export options in the respective tabs.

### How to Change Company Logo
1. Navigate to **Settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, click **Upload Logo**.
3. Select a PNG or JPEG file.
4. The logo will immediately update across the application.
